Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Cajun007 (the "App"), operated by Jason Roy, handles information obtained from TikTok.

Information we access

With your authorization, the App accesses only the connected TikTok account's:

  • Basic and profile information (open ID, display name, avatar, profile link, bio) — user.info.basic, user.info.profile
  • Account statistics (follower, following, like, and video counts) — user.info.stats
  • Public video list and video metadata — video.list
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account — video.upload, video.publish

How we use it

Information is used only to publish videos to the account owner's TikTok account and to display that account's videos and statistics to the account owner. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except as required by law. Videos you publish are sent to TikTok, which handles them under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely, are never shared, and are used only to call TikTok's APIs on the account owner's behalf. Data is retained only as long as needed to provide the App and is deleted when access is revoked or on request.

Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (or Manage app permissions). You may also request deletion of any stored data.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent via the TikTok account @cajun007.
